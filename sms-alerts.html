On-Call Alert Program

How HEY GIRL staff and volunteers receive emergency text alerts

H.E.Y. G.I.R.L. the Movement™ runs a small, internal emergency-alert system. When a girl or young woman presses the I Need Help Now button on hghey.org, our trained on-call team is notified by text message (SMS), email, and an automated phone call so someone can respond. Messages are sent from (682) 626-8121.

Who gets these messages

Only HEY GIRL staff members and volunteers who have enrolled as on-call responders. Typically 2 to 4 people are on rotation. We never send text messages to the public, to donors, or to the girls and women we serve.

How on-call responders enroll (opt in)

  1. During volunteer onboarding, the responder completes a written On-Call Responder Consent Form that includes their mobile number and this statement: "By providing my mobile number and signing below, I agree to receive emergency alert text messages from HEY GIRL (H.E.Y. G.I.R.L. the Movement) at the number provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes."
  2. The program director adds the number to the on-call list.
  3. The responder receives a one-time confirmation text: "HEY GIRL: You are enrolled in On-Call Alerts. Msg freq varies. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

What an alert looks like

HEY GIRL PANIC ALERT 11/03/2026, 2:14 PM CT Location: https://maps.google.com/?q=32.6499,-96.9083 Call 911 if there is immediate danger. Do not approach alone. Alert ID A1B2C3. Reply STOP to opt out.

Opt out, help, and costs

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving alerts. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages. This also removes you from the on-call rotation.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at hello@hghey.org or (972) 897-4277.
  • Message frequency varies and is low, usually a handful of alerts per month.
  • Message and data rates may apply.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Opt-in data and consent are never shared. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
