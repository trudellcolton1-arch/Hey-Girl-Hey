Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

H.E.Y. G.I.R.L. the Movement™ ("HEY GIRL," "we," "us") is a Texas nonprofit based in Duncanville, Texas, operating the website hghey.org. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we protect it. We are a domestic-violence and youth-empowerment ministry. Protecting the people who come to us is the whole point, so we collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Nothing by default. Browsing this site does not require an account. We do not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, or third-party embeds.
  • Emergency button ("I Need Help Now"). If you press the emergency button on our home page and allow location access, your device sends us your approximate GPS location plus anything you choose to type: your first name, a phone number, and a short note. All of those fields are optional. Your IP address is not stored.
  • Contact form and email. If you write to us, we keep what you send so we can reply.
  • Device storage. The site may store a small preference on your device (for example, that you dismissed the "add to home screen" banner). This never leaves your device.

How we use it

  • Emergency button data is used for one purpose only: to alert our trained on-call team so they can respond. It is delivered to them by email, text message, and automated phone call.
  • Emergency alerts are kept in a short-lived audit log for up to 24 hours, then deleted.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Ever.

Text messaging (SMS) and mobile information

HEY GIRL sends text messages only to staff members and volunteers who have personally enrolled in our On-Call Alert Program and given express written consent. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages to anyone, and we do not text program participants or the public.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Enrolled on-call team members may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, and may get help by replying HELP, emailing hello@hghey.org, or calling (972) 559-9717. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need to run the site and deliver alerts, each bound to use the data solely on our behalf: our hosting provider (Vercel), our email provider (Resend), and our telephone and SMS carrier (Telnyx). We may disclose information when required by law or to protect someone's safety.

Your choices

  • You can decline location access and still use the emergency button; the alert will say your location was not available.
  • You can use the Leave This Site button at any time. It opens a neutral page immediately. Clearing your browser history is still your responsibility.
  • You may ask us to delete anything you have sent us by emailing hello@hghey.org.

Children

Our programs serve girls and young women. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 online beyond what a child may voluntarily enter when asking for emergency help. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Alert data is transmitted to our providers over encrypted connections and is not stored on this website. No system is perfectly secure, which is why we collect so little.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new effective date. Questions: hello@hghey.org or (972) 559-9717. Mailing address: 115 E Davis St, Duncanville, TX 75116.

See also our Terms of Use and On-Call Alert Program.
