Emergency help

If you are in immediate danger, call 911. The "I Need Help Now" button notifies our volunteer on-call team. Mentorship, faith-based programming, referrals, and support for girls and young women. We are not a licensed counseling, legal, or medical provider. Content on this site is for information and encouragement, not professional advice.

On-Call Alert Program (text messaging terms)

Our On-Call Alert Program sends emergency text messages, emails, and automated phone calls to HEY GIRL staff and volunteers who have enrolled as on-call responders. Full program details are at hghey.org/sms-alerts.

  • Who receives messages: only enrolled staff and volunteers who gave express written consent during onboarding. We do not text the public or program participants.
  • What messages contain: emergency alerts triggered when someone presses the emergency button on our website, including a map link, an alert ID, and any optional details the person provided.
  • Frequency: varies. Alerts are rare, typically a handful of messages per month.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP to any message to stop receiving alerts. You will get one confirmation message. Replying STOP also removes you from the on-call rotation.
  • Help: reply HELP, email hello@hghey.org, or call (972) 897-4277.
  • Privacy: no mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
